# Suboptimal weight outcome risk calculator

Online calculator for the preoperative risk of suboptimal weight outcomes within 3 years after metabolic and bariatric surgery.

**Open the calculator:** https://mbs-risk-model.github.io/calculator/

## What it predicts

From seven routine preoperative variables (procedure, body mass index, age, sex, diabetes, glycated hemoglobin, and triglycerides), the calculator gives:

- the risk of suboptimal initial weight loss (total weight loss <20% at year 1);
- the risk of weight recurrence (regain of ≥20% of the maximal weight loss at year 2 or 3) in patients with total weight loss ≥20% at year 1;
- the risk of either outcome within 3 years.

The model was developed in 873 patients and validated unchanged in 453 patients operated on in an earlier period at the same center. External validation and recalibration should precede use in other settings. The calculator does not replace clinical judgment.

## Files

- `index.html`: the complete calculator (single file, no external dependencies). All calculations run in the browser; no data are sent or stored.
