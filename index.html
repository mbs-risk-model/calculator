About the model

Intended use

The calculator estimates, before surgery, the risk of the two patterns of suboptimal weight outcome, to support counseling and planning of follow-up intensity. It was developed in adults undergoing primary metabolic and bariatric surgery at a university hospital in China (development cohort 2020–2022, median BMI 37.9 kg/m²) and validated unchanged in patients operated on in 2015–2019 at the same center (3-year AUC 0.73; expected-to-observed ratio 1.08). External validation and recalibration should precede use in other settings. It does not replace clinical judgment.

Risk groups and decision curves

Risk groups are the tertiles of predicted 3-year risk in the development cohort (cut-offs 32.4% and 45.6%). In the temporal validation cohort, the observed 3-year incidence was 20.0% in the low, 36.3% in the intermediate, and 58.1% in the high risk group. In decision curve analysis, using the model to select patients for intensified follow-up gave a higher net benefit than following up all or no patients at risk thresholds from about 20%–25% up to 50%.

Annual risks of a first event among patients still at risk: logit h1 = 0.066961 + 1.949094 × LP; logit h2 = −0.497664 + 0.797452 × LP; logit h3 = 0.171756 + 0.797452 × LP.
Suboptimal initial weight loss = h1; weight recurrence = 1 − (1 − h2)(1 − h3); either outcome within 3 years = 1 − (1 − h1)(1 − h2)(1 − h3).

If triglycerides are not available, ln(triglycerides) = 0.2776 + 0.02976 × jejunojejunal bypass − 0.09305 × primary bypass − 0.01219 × BMI + 0.003803 × age + 0.1896 × male + 0.1054 × diabetes + 0.09118 × A1C (%).
Unit conversions: triglycerides mmol/L = mg/dL ÷ 88.57; A1C % = 0.09148 × mmol/mol + 2.152.
